# CodexNotch 0.2.2

- **Launch at login**: Start CodexNotch automatically when you log in to your Mac. Enable it in General settings.
- **More room for the menu bar**: Choose Off under Left side to hide the entire left area. Quota details remain available in the expanded card.
- **Clearer settings**: Preferences are grouped into General, Appearance, and Floating Bar, with concise descriptions in English and Chinese.
- **Floating-bar preview**: Try all four center styles and running, idle, or completed states without connecting an external display.
- **Independent animation control**: The app's animation switch now works independently of the system's Reduce Motion setting. Disabling animations reduces resource use while quota and task status continue updating.
- **Popup menu fix**: Remove redundant Settings-window activation that could interrupt the language and recent-task dropdown menus.

Users on 0.2.1 can update through Settings → Check for Updates. Users on 0.2.0 or earlier should install this version manually. Existing preferences are preserved.

DMG and ZIP downloads are available for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later. The app has not been notarized by Apple. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/fengdwx/codex-notch/blob/v0.2.2/README.md#install).
