# CodexNotch 1.0.0

CodexNotch 1.0.0 introduces built-in multi-account management and live standby quota comparison directly inside the notch and Settings.

- **Multi-account switching with shared workspace state:** Save multiple Codex logins locally (`0700`/`0600` vault) and switch from the notch or **Settings → Accounts**. All accounts keep sharing `~/.codex` projects and conversation history, while `.codex-global-state.json` workspace entries are synchronized across switches.
- **On-demand vertical account drawer:** With multiple accounts configured, the expanded notch collapses multi-account controls to a compact anchor bar showing available capacity and expands into a scrollable vertical account drawer (`Available`, `Recent`, and `All` filters for 5+ accounts) with inline confirmation.
- **Hover-live quota preview:** Hovering a standby account card previews its quota directly in the top bars and header ring without shifting card height, making it fast to compare remaining limits before switching.
- **Accurate window mapping & availability status:** Single-window 7-day plans map accurately to weekly quota instead of 5h, consumed 5h windows surface compact reset times side by side (`5h HH:mm · 7d MM-dd`), and signed-out or unavailable accounts are clearly flagged and excluded from healthy standby availability.

Update from Settings → Check for Updates, or download the DMG or ZIP. Existing preferences are preserved.

Downloads are available for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later. The app has not been notarized by Apple. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/fengdwx/codex-notch/blob/v1.0.0/README.md#install).
