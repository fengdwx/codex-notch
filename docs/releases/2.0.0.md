# CodexNotch 2.0.0

CodexNotch 2.0.0 delivers a redesigned macOS Settings interface, isolated browser OAuth sign-in, and hardened shared-home account switching.

- **Modernized Settings experience:** Replaces the multi-size tab shell with a fixed unified-chrome sidebar (`General`, `Appearance`, `External Island`, and `Accounts`), refined frameless notch icon, constant-contrast capsule switches honoring **Reduce Motion**, and quick **Check Updates** / **GitHub** links in the sidebar footer.
- **Browser-isolated OAuth sign-in:** Add or re-authenticate ChatGPT/Codex accounts in an isolated browser sandbox via **+ Sign in** without interrupting or signing out of your active Codex session.
- **Hardened shared-home switching:** Quits Codex before capturing rotated credentials with token freshness checks, detects conflicting standalone CLI/IDE processes safely, synchronizes project and New Chat continuation across switches, and rolls back automatically if relaunch fails.
- **Dual-row hover quota & visual polish:** Standby accounts show side-by-side 5h and 7d quota previews without shifting card height, running and completion cues render crisply across notch and external displays, and the external-screen customization tab is clearly titled **External Island**.

Update from Settings → Check Updates, or download the DMG or ZIP. Existing preferences are preserved.

Downloads are available for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later. The app has not been notarized by Apple. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/fengdwx/codex-notch/blob/v2.0.0/README.md#install).
