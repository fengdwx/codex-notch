# CodexNotch 2.1.0

CodexNotch 2.1.0 simplifies account onboarding in **Settings → Accounts** and brings the project documentation up to date.

- **Cleaner account onboarding:** Removed the obsolete external `CodexAccountSwitcher` auto-import and "Sync legacy" link. Add accounts cleanly via **+ Sign in**, **Import auth.json**, or **Adopt local login**.
- **Updated documentation:** Rewrote the English and Simplified Chinese READMEs to reflect multi-account shared-workspace workflows, **External Island** customization, privacy boundaries, and a new **Acknowledgments & References** section crediting open-source inspirations and dependencies.

Update from Settings → Check Updates, or download the DMG or ZIP. Existing preferences and saved accounts are preserved.

Downloads are available for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later. The app has not been notarized by Apple. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/fengdwx/codex-notch/blob/v2.1.0/README.md#install).
