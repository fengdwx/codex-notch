# CodexNotch 0.2.5

- **Quota ring alignment:** Fixed the rotating inner glint appearing off-center or doubled. The glint and halo stay centered on the quota ring, with crisp rendering on Retina displays.
- **Notch height:** Compact wings and both indicator lanes now follow the display's measured camera area. This change was previously available in the 0.2.4 preview.

Update from Settings → Check for Updates, or download the DMG or ZIP. Existing preferences are preserved.

Downloads are available for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later. The app has not been notarized by Apple. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/fengdwx/codex-notch/blob/v0.2.5/README.md#install).
