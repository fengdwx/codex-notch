# CodexNotch 0.2.6

- **Saved quota resets:** View available resets and their expiry from the expanded card. Use an eligible reset only after confirming the action.
- **Clearer activity feedback:** The floating bar distinguishes running, idle, and recent completion; the completion cue fades after about five seconds while conversation history remains available.
- **Refined floating styles:** Personal signatures feature a gentle four-bar activity indicator, while orbit, timer, and minimal light styles have clearer state cues.
- **Polished settings and quota display:** Improved layout, confirmation feedback, and readability.

Update from Settings → Check for Updates, or download the DMG or ZIP. Existing preferences are preserved.

Downloads are available for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or later. The app has not been notarized by Apple. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/fengdwx/codex-notch/blob/v0.2.6/README.md#install).
